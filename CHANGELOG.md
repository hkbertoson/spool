# spool

## 0.1.0

### Minor Changes

- First open-source release of Spool, a request board for the home 3D printer.

  - Request prints by link or description and follow them through Requested → Accepted → Printing → Ready for pickup, with comments and a progress timeline
  - Printer owners accept, advance (or drag on the board) and decline requests; requesters can withdraw until printing starts
  - History of finished, declined and withdrawn requests, filterable by outcome, material, text and time range
  - Invite only: owners invite and remove members from the Members page
  - Email notifications for new requests, status changes and comments, plus password reset
  - Email through Cloudflare Email Sending or Resend
